# Big Papa Games — brand and bot-profile image brief

## What Big Papa Games is

Big Papa Games is a cheerful collection of small, visual games that people can
open instantly from Telegram. Our mission is to bring joy to people's lives:
a welcome break in a busy day, the satisfaction of beating a personal best,
and the fun of a friendly challenge with a group.

Papa Chop is our first game, but the brand will grow to include many different
arcade games. The brand image should represent the whole collection, not one
game. Our personality is warm, playful, welcoming, confident, and a little cheeky.
Our message is **Little games. Big smiles.**

## Copy-and-paste image prompt

Create a square Telegram bot profile picture for **Big Papa Games**, a joyful
multi-game arcade whose mission is to bring joy to people's lives.

Feature **Big Papa**, a friendly, larger-than-life Black male cartoon mascot
with a broad build, a big warm smile, oversized sunglasses, a tilted gold crown,
a gold chain, and a vibrant purple-and-teal jacket. Give him the confident
1990s hip-hop style associated with Biggie, interpreted as a welcoming cartoon
arcade host. He should feel fun and approachable.

Use a close-up, chest-up composition with Papa holding a small game controller.
Add only a few simple arcade stars or playful geometric accents. Use bold dark
outlines, expressive shapes, and rich turquoise, purple, forest green, and
lemon-yellow colors. Make it look like polished, colorful 2D cartoon game art.

The icon must work as a small circular Telegram profile picture: center the
face, keep the crown and shoulders comfortably inside the circular safe area,
and use a simple high-contrast background. The expression and silhouette should
be recognizable at 48 pixels. Output a crisp 1024 × 1024 image.

**Do not include an axe, tree, chopping scene, or other imagery tied to a single
game. No text, lettering, watermarks, or existing company logos.**

## Optional matching banner

For a separate website/banner version, use the same mascot and palette in a
wider scene with a few different arcade-game motifs. If adding text, use only
**Big Papa Games** and **Little games. Big smiles.** Keep this separate from the
small profile icon so it stays readable.
